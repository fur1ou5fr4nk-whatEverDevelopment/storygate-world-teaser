What is StoryGate?

StoryGate connects Stories made by people with the places, objects and moments they belong to.

A invites one simple action: tap. You do not arrive at a feed or a wall of information. You step straight into an experience created for where you are—hear a voice, notice what others miss, follow a clue, make a choice or discover why something matters. The is not background to the Story. It becomes part of it.

What you find is not a disposable answer generated for the moment. StoryGate does not invent these Stories. People do—individuals and communities who have lived them, learned them, researched them or imagined them. Their gives each Story context, character and depth.

The first layer gives you what matters now. Then . Tap or hover selected words to reveal a memory, a source, another voice, a hidden connection or the next chapter. Go deeper when you want to. The Story never makes you dig when you do not.

For the people and communities behind a Story, one creation can welcome, explain, teach or inspire again and again. For everyone entering, a familiar place can become something worth seeing anew.

StoryGate is an early-stage startup, currently under development and testing. The first experiences are taking shape. The larger Story is still ahead.

Gate

A StoryGate marks the point where a place, object or moment opens into its Story. It can begin something brief—or the first step of a much larger journey.

World around you

The room, street, landscape, object and people around you are not scenery. They are part of what the Story asks you to notice.

Human perspective

Facts can be assembled. Perspective comes from the person who decides what matters, what connects and how the Story should unfold.

Curiosity takes over

One person may find what they need in a minute. Another can open sources, voices, clues or entire new chapters—without losing the thread.
