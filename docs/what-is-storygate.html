What is StoryGate?

StoryGate connects Stories made by people with the places, objects and moments they belong to.

A invites one simple action: tap. You do not arrive at a feed or a wall of information.

More about finding your way inShow less

There is no single best way into a Story. One person may want poetry; another a clear explanation or something they can use. StoryGate stays open to all of these paths, adapting the experience without flattening the Story itself.

Open does not mean without direction. StoryGate welcomes human experience while remaining guided by respect for people, legality and human dignity.

More about the boundariesShow less

Violence, exploitation and criminal activity are not supported. The platform stays open so that people can find their own way in—not so that harm can enter unchecked.

StoryGate is an early-stage startup, currently under development and testing. The first experiences are taking shape. The larger Story is still ahead.
